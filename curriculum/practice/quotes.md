# Distinguish a source from your reply

Quote both paragraphs of the reviewer’s feedback. Keep your final response outside the quote and mark the filename as code.

## Starter

~~~~markdown
Please include the setup command.

A clean checkout should work.

I added it to README.md.
~~~~

## Hint

The blank line inside a multi-paragraph quote can carry a quote marker.

## Worked solution

~~~~markdown
> Please include the setup command.
>
> A clean checkout should work.

I added it to `README.md`.
~~~~

## Self-check

The first two paragraphs share a quote boundary. The response has no quote bar, and the filename appears in monospace.
