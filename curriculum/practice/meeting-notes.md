# Turn meeting notes into actions

A docs team chose a text fallback for its launch video. Capture the decision, rationale, owner, due date and the question still open.

## Starter

~~~~markdown
Talked about video. Mira will do something before launch. Need review.
~~~~

## Hint

Use past tense for the decision. Give each future action a named owner and a date.

## Worked solution

~~~~markdown
# Launch video review — February 4

## Decision

Publish a descriptive transcript beside the launch video. Readers can retrieve the steps with playback disabled.

## Actions

- [ ] Mira — draft the transcript by February 6.
- [ ] Dev — compare it with the final clip by February 7.

## Open question

Which docs page should receive the first link? Dev will bring the two candidate pages to the next review.
~~~~

## Self-check

Someone who missed the meeting can separate the settled decision from the open question and identify the next owner.
