# Control paragraph and line breaks

Format the two address lines as one paragraph with a hard break. Put the delivery instruction in a new paragraph, emphasize Friday and keep the literal asterisks around FRAGILE.

## Starter

~~~~markdown
18 Hill Road
Pune
Deliver by Friday. Mark the box *FRAGILE*.
~~~~

## Hint

A visible backslash at the end of a line requests a hard break.

## Worked solution

~~~~markdown
18 Hill Road\
Pune

Deliver by **Friday**. Mark the box \*FRAGILE\*.
~~~~

## Self-check

The address occupies two lines without a paragraph gap. The instruction has a gap above it, Friday is bold and the two asterisks are visible.
